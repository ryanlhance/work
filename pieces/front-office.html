    How it works
    • Rows: last 14 days, last 30 days, season.
    • Colors rank each stat against the ~208 skaters or 32 goalies an 8-team league rosters. DEF ranks defensemen only.
    • OVR (1–99): overall value in your 10 categories. 50 = middle of rostered players.
    • Core top-half starter · Start lineup-worthy · Hold bench · Drop waiver-level.
    • Calls weigh last 30 days 60%, season 40% (goalies flipped).
    • Watch-list players sit under the roster player they'd replace (your weakest at that position). Upgrade, Even or Worse compares the two.
    • + moves a watch-list player to your team. − removes a player from the board. Changes stay on this page and clear on reload.
    • Faded cells = too few games to trust.
    • Under Hold: categories the player is strong in. Faded = strong lately only.
    • Targets: your weakest categories (from week 2, Swing categories first) and the best players not on your board for each, ranked by that category over season and last 30 days (OVR 40+). Goalie categories share one card.
    • 3Y: OVR over the last three seasons. A player with a thin season leans on it, so an injured star isn't called a Drop.
    • League: Lock won 70%+ of weeks · Swing in between · Chase 30% or less. From week 2, comparisons favor Swing categories.
